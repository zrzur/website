# Asset and source notices

This file lists the assets the website ships today. Earlier artwork (cartoon avatars, generated SVG product illustrations, old bitmap captures) is no longer part of the site and is not described here.

## Brand

- The Zrzur mark is the controller face, from the project's own logo files. The icons in `public/brand/` (favicons, app icons, maskable icons, share image, banner) and `public/assets/logo.svg` are generated from it. Nothing was redrawn.
- Zrzur is an independent product. Copyright headers, the `LICENSE` file and attribution for the open-source foundation stay intact. The marketing site does not brand Zrzur as Stoat or Revolt; AGPL source obligations are met by `LICENSE` and this notice.

## Product pictures

- `public/assets/product/` holds screenshots of the running Zrzur app in Arabic, English and German, light and dark, desktop and phone. They show demo accounts and demo content only, never real people's data. The language and theme are as captured; they are real captures, not a claim that every later app version looks identical.
