Zrzur is a place where friends and communities meet: servers with text and voice channels, screen sharing, roles and permissions, in your browser and on Windows, macOS and Linux. We want it to be a professional alternative to Discord that is free and open source.

## Where it started

It started with an open-source chat project we liked a lot. The idea was right and the foundation was solid. But its design did not win us over, and it was missing many things people need every day.

So we decided to take it and build our own product on it: a new design from the first screen to the last, a full Arabic experience that reads right to left, and features that make it a real competitor, not one more copy.

## What that means today

Every server has a welcome page with its icon, banner and description. Voice channels stay open: you join in a click and share your screen inside the call. Roles and permissions decide who reads, who posts and who moderates, and newcomers pick their interests on their first visit. Public servers can be found by topic. The app comes in many languages, and Arabic, English and German are checked line by line.

## What we keep

We build on open source and we keep it that way: the AGPL licence and the credit of the original contributors stay intact, and our code is public on GitHub.

## What comes next

Zrzur is at its beginning. This blog is where we explain what we build and why. Try it, and tell us what it is missing.
