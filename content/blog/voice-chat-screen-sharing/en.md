Text is good for keeping things. Voice is where a community comes alive: a late game night, a study group, a friend who wants to show you something on their screen. In Zrzur, all of this is free for everyone. This post explains how voice works, what you can set up, and what each setting does.

## Voice channels: always open

A voice channel in Zrzur is a room, not a call. Nobody has to ring anyone. You click the channel and you are in; you click leave and you are out. Everyone in the server can see who is talking, so it is easy to drop in when your friends are there.

In a call you can **mute** your microphone, **deafen** yourself so you hear nothing, and change the volume of each person on their own. You can also **turn on your camera**.

## Screen sharing with sound

Press **Share screen** in the call and choose a window, a tab or your whole screen. Your friends see it straight away. Tick **Share audio** and the **sound** goes with it, so a video or a game is heard too; if your browser cannot share sound from there, Zrzur tells you. Everyone watching can turn the stream's sound up, down or off on their own.

Screen sharing runs at **720p and 30 frames per second**, free for everyone. Hover over someone who is streaming to see a small preview before you open it.

## Stage channels for talks and events

Some evenings are not a conversation: a talk, a Q&A, a community meeting. For that there are **stage channels**. Speakers talk, listeners listen, and anyone in the audience can **ask to speak**. The people who run the stage decide who comes up.

## Soundboard: 115 sounds ready to use

The **soundboard** plays a short sound into the call for everyone: a drumroll for a winner, a cheer, a "ready, go" before a match. Every server has room for **8 sounds**. You can upload your own short clips or pick from a ready **library of 115 sounds** in six groups: voice, games, reactions, interface, alerts and coins. Each listener can turn the soundboard volume down in their own settings.

## A microphone that sends only what you mean

Good voice is mostly about the microphone. In **Settings › Voice** you will find:

- **Push to talk**: hold one key to talk instead of an open microphone. It works while Zrzur is the active window and you are not typing.
- **Input sensitivity**: your microphone sends sound only above the level you choose, so breathing and the room stay out.
- **Noise suppression**: off, the browser's own filter, or **Enhanced (RNNoise)**, which filters keyboard and fan noise on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere to be cleaned.
- **Echo cancellation**, so people do not hear themselves through your speakers.
- **Automatic gain control**, which keeps your microphone at a steady level.
- **High quality audio** for music: 128 kbps stereo, with the voice filters above turned off.

## Streaming? Hide what should stay private

If you stream your screen to other people, turn on **Streamer mode**. It hides your email address, your tag, invite links and codes, and the previews of direct messages in notifications, so they never show on stream. A small indicator reminds you that it is on.

## Where voice runs

Zrzur runs its own voice server, built on the open-source LiveKit software, in Frankfurt am Main, Germany. Your voice and video go to our server, not to an outside company. Voice works in any current browser and in the desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux.
