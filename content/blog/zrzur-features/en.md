Zrzur is a free place where friends and communities meet: servers with text and voice channels, screen sharing, roles and permissions, in your browser and on Windows, macOS and Linux. This post explains why we built it and walks through everything it can do today.

## Why we built Zrzur

Chat apps used to be simple: you made a server, invited your friends, and talked. Over the years, more and more of what made Discord fun moved behind a monthly subscription. Bigger uploads, sharper screen sharing, your own emoji everywhere, the look of your profile: one paid extra after another. We are not against paying for something good, but talking with your friends should not need a credit card.

We wanted a place where the everyday things are free for everyone: talking, calling, sharing your screen and running a community.

## Where Zrzur comes from

We did not start from an empty page. We found an open-source chat project with a solid foundation and a good idea behind it. We liked how it worked. We did not like how it looked, and it was missing a lot of what people use every day.

So we took its code and started building on it. We redesigned every screen, from the sign-in page to the last setting. We rebuilt the Arabic experience so it reads properly from right to left. And we added feature after feature until it became a full platform of its own. Zrzur is an independent product with its own design, releases and services.

The project we started from stays credited. Zrzur is open source under the AGPL licence, the licences and the names of the original contributors stay intact, and we do not claim their work as ours.

## Servers and channels

Every community gets a server, and every topic gets a place inside it.

- **Text channels and categories** keep everyday chat, announcements and shared files easy to follow.
- **Forum channels** hold longer discussions, each post with its own replies, sorted by tags.
- **Threads** take a side conversation out of a busy channel without losing it.
- **Messages** carry images, files, formatting, emoji and reactions. Each server can add its own emoji.

## Voice, video and screen sharing

- **Voice channels** are always open: you join in one click and leave when you want.
- **Screen sharing** from inside the call, with a preview when you hover over someone who is streaming.
- **Stage channels** for talks and events: speakers talk, listeners listen, and anyone can ask to speak.
- **Soundboard**: short sounds you play into the call, with a ready library of more than a hundred free sounds that any server can add.
- **Push-to-talk and voice sensitivity**, so your microphone sends only what you mean to say.

## A welcome that fits your community

- Every server has a **welcome page** with its icon, banner and description.
- On their first visit, newcomers **pick their interests** by answering a few questions, and get the matching roles at once. They can skip it and change it later.
- **Rules**: new members accept your server's rules when they join, and **join requests** let you approve members by hand.

## Roles, moderation and safety

- **Roles and permissions** decide who can read, who can write and who can moderate, down to each channel.
- **AutoMod** stops blocked words, mention spam, link spam and repeated messages: it blocks them, alerts your team or times the sender out.
- The **audit log** shows who changed what in your server.
- **Pause** invites and direct messages for a while when a server is under attack.
- **Server insights** show how your community grows: messages per channel, people who joined and left, and how many people write each day.
- Anyone can **report** a message, a profile or a server, and our team reviews it. Accounts of people aged 16 and 17 get direct messages only from their friends.

## Discover: find your community

**Discover** lists public servers and bots. You browse by topic or search by name and tag, read a server's page before you join, and join in a click. Server owners decide whether their server is listed.

## Profiles, Designs and badges

- Your **profile** has a banner, a short bio and badges that show what you have done on Zrzur.
- **Designs** is a gallery of decorations and nameplates made by creators in our community. You pick one and it shows on your profile and next to your name.

## Bots and apps

Developers can build **bots and apps** for Zrzur. A verified bot carries a clear tag, so people know who they are talking to. Bots appear in Discover, and server owners decide what each bot may do.

## Made for you

- **Dark or light**, with an accent colour you choose.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** for what you do most; press Ctrl+/ to see them all.
- **Streamer mode** hides your personal details while you stream.
- **Reduced motion** for anyone who prefers less animation.
- **Arabic, English and German** are checked line by line, Arabic fully from right to left, and the app speaks many more languages.

## Your account, your data

- **Two-step sign-in** and a list of your active sessions, which you can end at any time.
- **Download your data** whenever you want.
- **Delete your account** yourself: it locks at once and is erased 14 days later.
- We do not show anyone advertising based on profiling.

## Everywhere you are

Zrzur runs in any current browser, on a computer or a phone. For your computer there is a desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux on the [download page](/download).

## Free, and staying open

Everything in this post is free: there is nothing to pay in this version. Zrzur is at its beginning, and this blog is where we tell you what we build and why. [Open Zrzur](https://app.zrzur.com), make a server for your friends, and tell us what is missing.
