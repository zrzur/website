Running a chat platform costs money, every day. This post is honest about who pays for Zrzur, what it costs, where our limits are today, and how you can help, with or without money.

## An independent project

Zrzur is not a company with investors behind it. It is an **independent, non-commercial project** run from Austria by a small team. The costs of running it are paid **out of our own pocket**, and the work on the app, the servers and this website happens in our spare time.

That is a choice. We do not sell personal data, and we do not show anyone advertising based on profiling. The other side of that choice is simple: there is no advertising money to pay the bills.

## What it costs to run Zrzur

Every message, call and picture runs on real machines that someone pays for:

- **The servers** in Frankfurt am Main that run the app and the database.
- **The voice server** that carries every voice call, video and screen share.
- **Storage** for the images, files, emoji and sounds you share.
- **Domains, email and security**: zrzur.com and zrzur.cc, the service that sends verification and password emails, and the network protection that keeps attacks out.
- **Backups**, so a broken disk does not take your community with it.

## Our limits today

We would rather tell you than let you find out. Zrzur runs on a small setup of its own. That is enough for a growing community, but it is not endless:

- When many people arrive at once, things can be slower than they should be.
- To keep the service stable and keep spam out, we limit how many accounts can be created from one network address in a day.
- Upload sizes and stream quality are set to what we can carry today.

Support helps us carry more.

## Support Zrzur on Ko-fi

If Zrzur is useful to you or your community, you can support it on **[Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/zrzur)**, once or every month.

- **It is voluntary.** A donation unlocks no features in Zrzur and is not linked to your Zrzur account.
- **It goes to running Zrzur**: the servers, the voice server, storage, domains, email, security and backups.
- **Ko-fi handles the payment.** We never see your card or account details. What Ko-fi shares with us, and how we use it, is in our [privacy policy](/legal/privacy).
- Zrzur is a personal project, not a registered charity, so support is not tax-deductible.

## Help without money

Money is only one way. Most of what Zrzur needs is not money at all:

- **Bring your people.** Create a server for your friends or your community and invite them. A lively server helps more than anything else.
- **Tell us what is broken.** Write to support@zrzur.com with what you did and what happened. Every report helps us make Zrzur better.
- **Tell us what is missing.** Ideas from real communities shape what we build next.
- **Fix our words.** If a sentence in the app reads badly in your language, tell us how it should read.
- **Build a bot.** If you are a developer, build a bot or an app for Zrzur and submit it to Discover.
- **Share Zrzur.** A post, a message to a friend, a link from your website: people find Zrzur through people.

## Thank you

Every server that opens, every friend who joins and every coffee on Ko-fi keeps Zrzur running. Thank you for being part of it. If you have not tried it yet, [open Zrzur](https://app.zrzur.com), or read [what Zrzur can do](/blog/discord-alternative) first.
