Zrzur is free for everyone: talking, calling, sharing your screen and running a community cost nothing. But running a chat platform does cost money, every day. This post is honest about who pays for Zrzur, what it costs, where our limits are today, and how you can help, with or without money.

## An independent project

Zrzur is not a company with investors behind it. It is an **independent, non-commercial project** run from Austria by a small team. The costs of running it are paid **out of our own pocket**, and the work on the app, the servers and this website happens in our free time.

That is a choice. We do not show advertising, we do not build profiles of you to sell ads, and nothing in the app is there to make you stay longer than you want. The other side of that choice is simple: there is no advertising money to pay the bills.

## What it costs to run Zrzur

Every message, call and picture runs on real machines that someone pays for:

- **The servers** in Frankfurt am Main that run the app, the database and the website.
- **The voice server** that carries every voice call, video and screen share.
- **Storage** for the images, files, emoji and sounds you share.
- **Domains, email and security**: zrzur.com and zrzur.cc, the service that sends verification and password emails, and the network protection that keeps attacks out.
- **Backups**, so a broken disk never takes your community with it.

## Our limits today

We would rather tell you than let you find out. Zrzur runs on **one main server and one voice server**. That is enough for a growing community, but it is not endless:

- When many people arrive at once, things can be slower than they should be.
- To keep the service stable and keep spam out, we limit how many accounts can be created from one address per day.
- Some features we would like to offer, such as bigger uploads and sharper streams, cost more than we can carry today, so they are not switched on yet.

As more people support Zrzur, these limits move.

## Support Zrzur on Ko-fi

If Zrzur is useful to you or your community, you can support it on **[Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/zrzur)**, once or every month.

- **It is voluntary.** Supporting Zrzur unlocks no features, and Zrzur stays free for everyone either way.
- **It goes to running Zrzur**: the servers, the voice server, storage, domains, email, security and backups.
- **Ko-fi handles the payment.** We never see your card or account details. What Ko-fi shares with us, and how we use it, is in our [privacy policy](/legal/privacy).
- Zrzur is a personal project, not a registered charity, so support is not tax-deductible.

## Help without money

Money is only one way. Most of what Zrzur needs costs you nothing:

- **Bring your people.** Create a server for your friends or your community and invite them. A full server helps more than anything else.
- **Tell us what is broken.** Write to support@zrzur.com with what you did and what happened. Every report makes Zrzur better for everyone.
- **Tell us what is missing.** Ideas from real communities decide what we build next.
- **Fix our words.** If a sentence in the app reads badly in your language, tell us how it should read.
- **Build a bot.** If you are a developer, build a bot or an app for Zrzur and add it to Discover.
- **Share Zrzur.** A post, a message to a friend, a link from your website: people find Zrzur through people.

## Thank you

Every server that opens, every friend who joins and every coffee on Ko-fi keeps Zrzur running and free. Thank you for being part of it. If you have not tried it yet, [open Zrzur](https://app.zrzur.com), or read [what Zrzur can do](/blog/discord-alternative) first.
